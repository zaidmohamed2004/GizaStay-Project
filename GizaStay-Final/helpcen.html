Stays
How do I book a stay?

Search a destination on the home page, open a property, choose your dates and guests, then select Reserve. You confirm guest details and pay to finish.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Open Profile then Trips, select the booking and choose Cancel Booking. Whether cancellation is free depends on the property policy shown at checkout.

When am I charged?

Payment is taken at the payment step. Some stays offer paying part now and part later; the amounts and dates are shown before you confirm.

Loyalty & Rewards
How do I earn rewards?

You earn points automatically on completed stays booked while signed in to your account.

Do my points expire?

Points stay valid for 24 months from your most recent completed booking.

Security
How do I keep my account secure?

Use an email address you control and never share your verification code. GizaStay will never ask for that code by phone or email.

I received a login code I did not request

You can ignore it; the code cannot be used by anyone else. Sign in and review your recent activity in your profile.

Things to do
Can I book activities through GizaStay?

Activities listed on a property page are informational in this version. Arrange them with the property after check-in.

Are activity prices per person?

Yes, activity prices are shown per person unless the listing states otherwise.

Refunds & Charges
How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Why was I charged a service fee?

The GizaStay service fee covers support and secure payment. It is always shown in the price breakdown before you pay.

I see a charge I do not recognise

Check Profile then Trips for the matching booking, then contact support with the reservation code.

Account
How do I update my details?

Go to Profile then Personal Data, edit your name, phone, address or photo, and select Save changes.

How do I delete my account?

Profile then Settings then Delete account removes your local data from this device.

Privacy
What data does GizaStay store?

In this project your login state, profile details, wishlist and bookings are stored only in your browser using localStorage.

How do I clear my data?

Log out to clear session data, or use Profile then Settings then Delete account to remove everything.

Travel Alerts
Where do I see travel alerts?

Important destination notices appear on the property page and again before checkout.

Do alerts affect my cancellation options?

If an official travel alert affects your dates, contact support with your reservation code to review the options.
